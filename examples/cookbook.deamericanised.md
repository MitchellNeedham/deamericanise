# From The International Jewish Cook Book (1919)

## Plum Pudding (For Thanksgiving Day)

Soak a small loaf of bread; press out every drop of water, work into this **240 millilitres** of suet shaved very fine, the yolks of six eggs, **240 millilitres** of currants, **240 millilitres** of raisins seeded, **120 millilitres** of citron shredded fine, **180 millilitres** of syrup, one wineglass of brandy, **240 millilitres** of sifted flour and the stiffly-beaten whites of eggs last. Boil four hours in greased melon **mould**. PLUM PUDDING, No. 2 Chop a half box of raisins and currants, **110 grams** of citron, **110 grams** of suet (chopped very fine), two eggs, **350 millilitres** of sugar, a wineglass of brandy, **470 millilitres** of cider, **4.9 millilitres** of cinnamon and ground cloves. When all these are well mixed add enough flour (with a teaspoon of baking-powder in it) to thicken well. Cook in a greased **mould** and allow to steam for three hours.

## Nut Honey Cake

Mix **470 millilitres** of brown sugar, **470 millilitres** of honey, six egg yolks and beat them thoroughly. Sift together **710 millilitres** of flour, **1.2 millilitres** of salt, **15 millilitres** of ground cinnamon, **2.5 millilitres** each of ground cloves, ground nutmeg and allspice, and **7.4 millilitres** of soda; add **240 millilitres** of chopped raisins, **14 grams** of citron cut in small pieces, **14 grams** of candied orange peel cut in small pieces, **230 grams** of almonds coarsely chopped. Beat the whites of three eggs very stiff and add them last. Pour the dough to the depth of about half an inch into well-buttered tins and bake in a slow oven for one-half hour. *ICINGS AND FILLINGS FOR CAKES*

## Fruit Wheels

Sift together **470 millilitres** of flour, **9.9 millilitres** of baking-powder, **2.5 millilitres** of salt, **15 millilitres** of sugar. Rub in two large tablespoons of butter. Mix to soft dough with milk; roll out **1.3 centimetres** thick. Spread thickly with soft butter, dust with **4.9 millilitres** of flour, **59 millilitres** of granulated sugar, **4.9 millilitres** of cinnamon; sprinkle over **120 millilitres** each of seeded and cut raisins, chopped citron, and cleaned currants. Roll up, cut in **2.5 centimetre** slices, put **2.5 centimetres** apart on greased, flat pans, and bake in hot oven. *PANCAKES, FRITTERS, Etc.*

## Fruit Cake (Wedding Cake)

Take **450 grams** of butter and **450 grams** of sugar rubbed to a cream, yolks of twelve eggs, **15 millilitres** of cinnamon, **4.9 millilitres** of allspice, **2.5 millilitres** of mace, **2.5 millilitres** of cloves, one-fourth of a pound of almonds pounded, **910 grams** of raisins (seeded and chopped), **1.4 kilograms** of currants (carefully cleaned), **450 grams** of citron (shredded very fine), and one-quarter of a pound of orange peel (chopped very fine). Soak all this prepared fruit in **470 millilitres** of brandy overnight. Add all to the dough and put in the stiffly-beaten whites last. Bake in a very slow oven for several hours, in cake pans lined with buttered paper. When cold wrap in cloths dipped in brandy and put in earthen jars. If baked in gas oven have light very low. Keep oven the same temperature for four or five hours.

## Sour Milk Biscuits

Mix and sift **470 millilitres** of flour, **2.5 millilitres** of salt and **2.5 millilitres** of soda; cut in **15 millilitres** of butter, stir in with a knife enough sour milk to make a soft dough. Roll **1.3 centimetres** thick; cut in small rounds and bake in a quick oven about twenty minutes. MUFFINS. Light the burners of the gas oven before beginning to mix the muffins and work rapidly. Place in a mixing-bowl one well-beaten egg, **30 millilitres** of butter, **15 millilitres** of sugar, **2.5 millilitres** of salt, one scant cup of milk and **9.9 millilitres** of baking-powder that have been sifted with sufficient flour to form a batter that will "ribbon" from the spoon. Beat the batter steadily for five minutes, stir in **15 millilitres** of melted butter and bake in muffin-pans in a quick oven. These muffins will bake in ten minutes if pans are only half filled.

## Reception Cocoa

Stir **240 millilitres** of boiling water gradually onto **30 millilitres** of cocoa, **30 millilitres** of sugar and **4.9 millilitres** of cornstarch, a few grains of salt (that have been well mixed) in a saucepan; let boil five minutes, stirring constantly. Heat **710 millilitres** of milk in a double boiler, add the cocoa mixture and **2.5 millilitres** of vanilla; beat with egg-beater until foamy and serve hot in chocolate cups, with a tablespoon of whipped cream on top of each cup, or take the cheaper marshmallows, place two in each cup and fill cups two-thirds full of hot cocoa.

## Mixed Pickles

Wash **950 millilitres** of large cucumbers, cut in cubes, **950 millilitres** of small cucumbers left whole, **950 millilitre** small silver-skinned onions, **950 millilitre** small green tomatoes chopped coarse, two red peppers chopped fine, one large cauliflower broken in small pieces; pour over them a weak brine solution made of **950 millilitres** of water and a cup of salt. Let stand twenty-four hours; bring to a boil in same solution, drain and make the dressing. *Mixed Pickle Dressing.*--Mix **89 millilitres** of mustard, **15 millilitres** of turmeric, **240 millilitres** of flour, **470 millilitres** of sugar and **1.9 litres** of vinegar. These ingredients must be thoroughly mixed and then cooked until thick. Stir in the pickles; heat thoroughly; empty into glass jars and stand away until needed.
