Brown Butter Pumpkin Bread

By Jordan Ellis · October 14, 2025 · 15 minutes prep

This is my favorite fall bake. The color comes from the pumpkin and the flavor from browning the butter until it smells like toffee. It keeps for a week, if it lasts that long in your neighborhood.

Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9-inch loaf pan. Whisk the flour with the spices, then fold in the pumpkin, sugar and eggs. Bake for 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Don't worry if the center dips a little as it cools.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 ounces) pumpkin puree
  • 1 stick butter, browned until it turns a deep amber color
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar and 2 large eggs

Method

  1. Brown the butter over medium heat, swirling until it smells nutty, then let it cool.
  2. Line the pan with parchment or aluminum foil so the loaf lifts out in one piece.
  3. Fold everything together gently. Overmixing makes the crumb tough and gray.
